\begin{center}
    \textbf{List of revisions made to ``On Vassiliev Invariants and Weight Systems of Classical and Welded Knots''}
\end{center}

\vspace{1.5cm}

There are two reports for this thesis, a report by Audoux and a report by an anonymous reviewer.

The report by Audoux contains issues to be corrected / suggestions, of which six are identified as major, and twenty-nine are identified as minor. The report also lists some possible typos for correction. We will reference these issues as Audoux.M1 through Audoux.M6 for the major issues, and Audoux.m1 through Audoux.m29 for the minor issues.

The report by Raynor contains two general required revisions in Section 2.1, a list of required revisions by section of the thesis, and a list of recommendations for future publication of the thesis, also by subsection of the thesis. We refer to the revisions in this report by the sections of the report, then by the sections of the thesis which they address.

        Auduou.m4: why introduce the notation \(Q'\) when it's soon shown to be equal to \(P = \partial Q\)?
        & For the same reason we use the notation \(f\) and \(F\) when learning/proving the fundamental theorem of calculus: not to confuse the notation by a choice which would suggest the hypothesis. Also, to be consistent with the literature, specifically Willterton, which is cited.\\
        \hline

        Auduox.m5: Remark 1.2.18 is intriguing but uinnformative
        & Indeed, it is an open question to explain this, so there is nothing informative to say. However I feel that this intriguing point deserves to be stated.\\
        \hline

        Audoux.m11: why use the words covariant and contravariant?
        & They are standard enough, and could be useful for readers of less categorical backgrounds. Furthermore they are used a few more times in similar ways in the thesis.\\
        \hline

        Audoux.m29: ``does it means that the universal welded weight system constructed in Section 3.4 is in some sense contained in \(\mathfrak{gl}_{1|1}\) in some way?''
        & Yes, in some way. This is mentioned in the final paragraph (and additionally a reference is now included).\\
        \hline

        Audoux.M4: concatenation of welded long knots as an example of definitions appearing late or not at all.
        & I accept and appreciate this point in general (all other examples listed, and some not listed have led to changes), but I think this is acceptable --- for example some basic knot theory notions are also missing in Chapter 1, and the audience is assumed to know some basic topology. Other than this specific instance, the rest of the suggestions of Audoux.M4 were implemented.\\
        \hline

        Audoux.m16: ``I am intrigued by assumption (e)...`` (the \(S_{n}\) action in the definition of an operad).
        & I still believe this to be correct. There is already an \(S_{n}\) action generated by \(\tau\) from the symmetric monoidal category \(\mathcal{C}\). Futhermore, the other examiner who is an expert in this topic had no qualms with this definition.\\
        \hline

        Raynor.typo2: ``...are used but not identified.''
        & These are consistent with the notation for the rest of Chapter.\\
